# Audio licenses

All local sound effects in `public/audio/` come from Kenney's CC0 audio packs.
They are used locally and are not requested from a third-party host at runtime.

- Creator: Kenney

- License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Source credits: https://github.com/Cy4nWare/sfx-api/blob/main/CREDITS.md
- Source catalog: https://github.com/Cy4nWare/sfx-api/blob/main/catalog.json

Files used:

- `metal-light-000.ogg`, `metal-light-001.ogg`, `metal-light-002.ogg`: light metal impacts for starter attempts and workshop adjustments.
- `metal-click.ogg`: ignition/contact click and final timing click.
- `metal-latch.ogg`: short mechanical latch/fit sounds during the repair sequence.

The files are unchanged CC0 source samples. Playback applies moderate gain and
subtle stereo panning in the browser to keep the narrative mix restrained.
